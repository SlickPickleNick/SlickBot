Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 • SlickBot Platform

1. Information We Collect

SlickBot collects minimal, essential data required to provide its community management features:

  • Server Metadata: Discord Server ID, Server Name, Channel IDs, Role IDs, and configured timezone.
  • Member Progression: Member IDs, Level XP, Voice minutes logged, and achievement unlocks.
  • Audit & Moderation Records: Moderation case IDs, action types (warn/mute/kick/ban), timestamps, and staff actor tags.
  • Support Transcripts: Ticket history and application responses stored within your server's designated log channels.

2. How Data is Used

All collected data is used exclusively to facilitate bot commands, render rank cards, synchronize live social alerts, and maintain staff audit trails. We do not sell, monetize, or share member data with third parties or external advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL database clusters with strict multi-tenant partitioning by guild_id. Only authorized server administrators can access their server's data via Discord OAuth2 authentication.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Server owners have full sovereignty over their data. Running /reset or removing the bot from your Discord server halts data collection and allows full data archival or purge upon request.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in our bot release notes and changelog channels.
